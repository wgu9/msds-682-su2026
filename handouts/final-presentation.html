Format

Make the shortest clear case for your project

Individual

5 + 1

Five minutes presenting, then one minute for Q&A.

Two-person team

7 + 1

Seven minutes presenting, then one minute for Q&A. Both students must speak.

Recommended five-slide elevator pitch

Five slides are recommended, not required. An equivalent concise structure is acceptable.

1

Problem

Target user and useful decision.

2

Data

Source and Kafka event contract.

3

System

Streaming architecture and processor.

4

Evidence

Result and validation or evaluation.

5

AI + lesson

Role, verification, limitation, or next step.

Live demo is optional. A recorded result or submitted artifact is acceptable. Do not spend the presentation walking through files or reading code.

Presentation bonus · up to 3 points

Three optional ways to go beyond the base story

+1 · Design tradeoff

Name a credible alternative, the constraint, and why you chose the implemented design.

+1 · Evidence visual

Use one accurate visual, table, or concise demonstration to connect a claim to evidence.

+1 · Technical defense

Answer the focused Q&A question with project evidence and appropriate uncertainty.

Maximum presentation score: 23. Bonus cannot replace a missing base category.
