# Final Presentation Peer-Review Extra Credit

Complete reviews of **three different projects presented by other students** to
earn up to **2 extra-credit points**. This activity is optional and does not
replace the final presentation or written-package requirements.

## What Counts as One Complete Review

For each selected project, write three short, specific items:

1. **Evidence-based strength:** Name one effective design choice, result, or
   piece of evidence shown in the presentation.
2. **Focused question or limitation:** Identify one question, assumption,
   limitation, or risk that matters to the result.
3. **Actionable suggestion:** Recommend one realistic clarification, check, or
   next step.

Generic comments such as "good presentation" do not count as a complete review.
Review three distinct projects; do not review your own project or your teammate's
project.

## Atomic Scoring Rule

| Completed reviews meeting all three conditions | Extra credit |
|---:|---:|
| **3** | **+2 points** |
| **2** | **+1 point** |
| **1** | **+0.5 point** |
| **0** | **0 points** |

## Submission

Submit all reviews in one PDF or Markdown file through the Canvas peer-review
submission location by **Friday, August 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM PDT**. Use this
filename:

```text
final_peer_reviews_<usf_username>.pdf
```

Equivalent `.md` submission is acceptable. Each review must identify the
project title. Keep feedback concise, respectful, and useful to the presenting
students.

Peer-review extra credit is scored individually, including for students who
completed the final project in a two-person team.
